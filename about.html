About the lab

Structural bioinformatics, computational chemistry and multicomponent synthesis converge to design and understand bioactive molecules.

Mission

Design molecules with a mechanistic understanding of their biological interactions.

SB²CS develops and studies innovative bioactive molecules by combining multicomponent reactions with structural bioinformatics. Computational models are used to characterize molecular recognition and guide experimentally accessible chemical structures.

Vision

Advance medicinal organic chemistry through integrated computational and synthetic research.

The laboratory seeks to contribute to the discovery of compounds with therapeutic potential while strengthening the connection between molecular modeling, chemical synthesis, biological interpretation and interdisciplinary training.

Research cycle

Iterative by design.

01

Explore

Identify molecular targets, chemical spaces and reaction opportunities with biological relevance.

02

Model

Analyze ligand–receptor interactions, conformational behavior and energetic trends.

03

Synthesize

Generate molecular diversity through efficient synthetic strategies and multicomponent reactions.

04

Refine

Use experimental and computational evidence to improve the next generation of candidates.

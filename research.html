Research

From molecular recognition to synthetic accessibility, we connect computation and experiment to explore therapeutic chemical space.

01

Structural bioinformatics

Atomic-level analysis of ligand–receptor recognition and conformational behavior.

  • Molecular docking and consensus screening
  • Molecular dynamics and enhanced sampling
  • Affinity and free-energy analysis
  • Protein–ligand interaction profiling
02

Multicomponent synthesis

Efficient construction of chemical diversity through reactions such as Ugi, Passerini, Biginelli and related strategies.

  • Reaction-space exploration
  • Mechanistic analysis
  • Rapid generation of diverse scaffolds
  • Synthetic accessibility as a design constraint
03

Bioactive compound design

Integrated computational and synthetic design of compounds with potential activity on biologically relevant targets.

  • GTPases and kinases associated with cancer
  • Ligand optimization and prioritization
  • ADMET-aware molecular design
  • Iterative computational–experimental refinement

Research principle

Use computation to ask better questions, synthesis to test them, and evidence to iterate.
